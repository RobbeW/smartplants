Project Smart Plants

Build a smart plant meter.

The Smart Plants Project has students measure soil moisture and calibrate a sensor to build a plant monitor.

Start platform View the assignment

How does it work?

Build, collect data and make your first report

Students first predict what will happen. Then they build the setup, calibrate their sensor and collect measurements in the browser.

1

Predict

Students write down what they expect with dry, moist and wet soil.

2

Calibrate

The class measures what dry, moist and wet mean for this sensor.

3

Measure

The browser shows live how the sensor value changes during the measurement.

4

Design

Choose when the plant needs water and let an output react.

5

Report

Download the measurement data or create a PDF with graph, conclusion and reflection.

The setup

Get started with sensors

The capacitive sensor measures how the electrical capacitance around it changes. More water in the potting soil changes that value. The Arduino reads the raw value.

  • Capacitive soil-moisture sensor, for example AZ-Delivery V1.2.
  • Arduino Uno with USB data cable.
  • School laptop with Chrome or Edge.
  • Plant in potting soil and water for the measurement.

In pictures

All the information at a glance

Students work with real plants, sensors and their microcontroller. They immediately see the measurements and changes on their laptop screen.

What will you learn?

What do students practise?

01

Measuring with sensors

A sensor gives a number. By measuring dry and wet potting soil, students learn what that number means in their own experiment.

02

Calibrating

They compare dry, lightly moist and wet soil before choosing a threshold.

03

IPO thinking

They recognize input, processing and output in their own circuit.

04

Investigating

They compare measurement series and write a conclusion with their own data.

For teachers

Ready to use in class.

The assignment is made for students around 13-14 years old. Start with demo mode, then connect the hardware and let students finish with a CSV file or PDF report.

  • Suitable for short research moments in Computer Science or STEM.
  • Usable with Arduino, micro:bit code or demo mode only.
  • PDF export bundles hypothesis, calibration, measurement series, graph, conclusion and reflection.
  • No backend needed: the folder can run as a static site on GitHub Pages.
